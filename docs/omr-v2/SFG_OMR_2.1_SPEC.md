# SFG OMR LAB - OMR Sheet Layout Specification

Machine-readable layout contract for the SFG OMR 2.1 sheet (`SFG_OMR_2.1.pdf`,
primary grey-outline variant), generated by `generate_sfg_omr_2_1.py`. The
same constants produce the PDF, this document and the
`SFG_OMR_2.1_COORDINATES.json` contract, so the files can never drift apart.
All coordinates are in millimetres.

## 1. Overview

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Layout version | 2.1 (v2.0 geometry preserved; calibration furniture upgraded) |
| Page | A4 portrait, 210.0 x 297.0 mm |
| Coordinate origin | bottom-left corner of the page; x right, y up |
| Ink model | black form ink; bubble outlines light grey #A6A6A6 (variant: pale blue #8CAAE6); interiors white |
| Registration markers | 8 solid black squares: 4 corner 7 x 7 mm + 4 edge-midpoint 4 x 4 mm (unchanged) |
| ArUco markers | 4, DICT_4X4_50 IDs 0-3, 7 mm (new in v2.1, optional registration aid, see section 7) |
| Timing marks | 50 solid black rectangles (25 row positions x 2 margins), 2.2 x 1.6 mm (unchanged) |
| Calibration strip | 7 patches 8 x 6 mm: 0 / 12.5 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 87.5 / 100%% grey (moved left, see section 6) |
| White references | 2 outlined 6 x 6 mm white squares: top, bottom (MIDDLE removed in v2.1) |

## 2. Coordinate system

Every coordinate below is expressed in millimetres with the origin at the
bottom-left corner of the A4 page: x increases to the right, y increases
upwards. Bubble positions are given as **centres**. This matches the
ReportLab canvas convention, so PDF points are simply `mm * 72 / 25.4`.

## 3. Design decisions

- **Full rebuild.** The sheet was redesigned from scratch, not patched: the
  vertical budget is now header 37.5 mm + test-ID -26.0 mm + answer grid -196.0 mm
  + footer ~15 mm, with every section separated by a full-width rule so no
  dead band remains between test-ID and the answer grid.
- **Row pitch.** The brief's 5.5-5.8 mm pitch target assumes a denser grid
  than the mandated two-column layout can produce. With two columns of 25
  rows, honouring the 'use the entire available vertical area' instruction
  and the 195-205 mm grid allocation requires a pitch of **7.4 mm**.
  Larger pitch also improves bubble separation in phone photographs, which
  the brief prioritises over decoration.
- **Bubble geometry.** 4.0 mm outline circles, 0.28 mm stroke, uniform
  centres per option column; no decorative circles, no fills, no boxes
  around rows.
- **Machine-first styling.** Embedded FreeSans/FreeSansBold (vector), 6-15 pt
  labels, vector elements only, plain white background, no decorative ink.
- **v2.0 ink model (geometry preserved).** All bubble outlines are printed
  in light grey #8CAAE6 at 0.32 mm stroke so student pen marks are darker than
  the preprinted outline; interiors stay white and unshaded. The grey was
  chosen empirically (15 candidate greys x strokes rendered at 300/150 dpi,
  scripts/test_gray_candidates.py): visible against paper (contrast ~78 of
  255), still above the page Otsu threshold (132) after a worst-case -12%%
  print-density loss, and clearly lighter than ballpoint-pen ink. An
  experimental pale-blue outline (#8CAAE6, luminance-matched to the grey)
  exists as a variant for RGB-camera tests; see the engineering report for
  its limitations (no gain on monochrome printers or grayscale pipelines).
- **v2.0 calibration additions.** The calibration strip moved to the bottom
  margin with five evenly spaced tones (0/25/50/75/100%%) for tone-curve
  estimation, and three outlined white reference squares (top / middle /
  bottom) sample local paper brightness. No alignment-critical coordinate
  moved: marker, timing-mark, bubble, test-ID, QR and writing-field
  geometry is byte-identical to v1.1.
- **v2.1 calibration upgrades (marking space untouched).** The calibration
  strip gains two end tones (12.5%% and 87.5%%; seven patches, box moved to
  x 128 - 190). The MIDDLE white reference was removed: its outlined-but-
  empty square at the centre of the answer grid read as an accidental blank
  box, and TOP/BOTTOM refs plus the strip's WHITE patch fully cover
  paper-white normalisation. Four 7 mm ArUco markers (DICT_4X4_50, IDs 0-3)
  were added in the free margin slots as an optional registration aid. Every
  bubble, marker, timing-mark, test-ID, QR and writing-field coordinate is
  byte-identical to v2.0.
- **v1.1 heritage.** Bubble diameter 3.4 mm on a 7.4 mm pitch (UPSC-style
  proportions), 8 registration markers and 50 timing marks carried over
  unchanged from v1.1.

## 4. Registration markers

Solid black squares, all inset 6 mm from the page edges: four corner
markers of 7 x 7 mm (primary perspective references, v1.0) and four
smaller edge-midpoint markers of 4 x 4 mm added in v1.1 at the
midpoints of the four page edges for rotation / scale verification.

| Marker | Kind | Size (mm) | Box x-range | Box y-range | Centre (x, y) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TOP_LEFT | corner | 7 | 6 - 13 | 284 - 291 | (9.5, 287.5) |
| TOP_RIGHT | corner | 7 | 197 - 204 | 284 - 291 | (200.5, 287.5) |
| BOTTOM_LEFT | corner | 7 | 6 - 13 | 6 - 13 | (9.5, 9.5) |
| BOTTOM_RIGHT | corner | 7 | 197 - 204 | 6 - 13 | (200.5, 9.5) |
| TOP_CENTER | edge_midpoint | 4 | 103 - 107 | 287 - 291 | (105.0, 289.0) |
| BOTTOM_CENTER | edge_midpoint | 4 | 103 - 107 | 6 - 10 | (105.0, 8.0) |

| RIGHT_CENTER | edge_midpoint | 4 | 200 - 204 | 146 - 150 | (202.0, 148.5) |

No printed element may come within 1.5 mm of any marker box; the generator
asserts this on every run. After rectification, the edge-midpoint centres
must land at x = 105.0 (top / bottom) and y = 148.5 (left / right).

v2.0 quiet-zone report: the JSON records the MEASURED white space around
every marker (`registration_markers[].clearance_mm`). The corner markers
keep >= 3 mm of clear paper on all sides and the edge-midpoint markers
>= 2 mm; no v2.0 element was placed near any marker, so the quiet zones
were documented without moving any ink.

## 6. Calibration strip (seven tones, extended in v2.1)

Seven 8 x 6 mm patches with a 0.3 mm black outline and 1 mm gaps,
0 / 12.5 / 25 / 50 / 75 / 87.5 / 100%% grey for tone-curve estimation, in the
bottom margin (strip box x 128 - 190, y 7.5 - 13.5). The strip moved 18 mm
left of its v2.0 position to fit the seventh patch; it is calibration-only
and is NOT used for alignment, so no fixed scanner coordinate depends on it.
All greys are neutral (R=G=B). The two added end tones tighten the shadow
(12.5%%) and highlight (87.5%%) ends of the printer tone curve, where the
v2.0 five-step curve was sparsest.

| Patch | Grey level | Target 8-bit luminance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BLACK | 0% | 0 - 40 |
| GREY_12.5 | 12.5% | 24 - 48 |
| GREY_25 | 25% | 49 - 79 |
| GREY_50 | 50% | 113 - 143 |
| GREY_75 | 75% | 176 - 206 |
| GREY_87.5 | 87.5% | 208 - 232 |
| WHITE | 100% | 235 - 255 |

## 7. ArUco markers (new in v2.1)

Four 7 mm ArUco markers (OpenCV DICT_4X4_50, IDs 0-3; 6 x 6 module grid at
1.167 mm per module including the one-module white border) sit in the
formerly empty margin slots. They are an OPTIONAL registration aid: the four
7 mm corner squares and four 4 mm edge-midpoint squares (section 4) remain
the primary references and the rectification pipeline does not change. ArUco
gives the scanner a redundancy path when corner markers are occluded
(staples, fold damage) and unambiguous sheet-side identification. Detected
corners must land within +-0.5 mm of the nominal centres below.

| Marker | ArUco ID | Box x-range | Box y-range | Centre (x, y) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ARUCO_TOP_A | 0 | 80 - 87 | 283.5 - 290.5 | (83.5, 287.0) |
| ARUCO_TOP_B | 1 | 148 - 155 | 283.5 - 290.5 | (151.5, 287.0) |
| ARUCO_BOT_A | 2 | 48 - 55 | 6.5 - 13.5 | (51.5, 10.0) |
| ARUCO_BOT_B | 3 | 114 - 121 | 6.5 - 13.5 | (117.5, 10.0) |

Each marker keeps >= 3.5 mm of clear paper on every side, so detection works
without extra padding. The module bit matrices are stored in
`aruco_markers[].bits` in the JSON.

## 8. White paper references (v2.1: middle removed)

Two outlined 6 x 6 mm white squares (0.25 mm black outline, bare-paper
interior) let the scanner sample local paper brightness near the top and
bottom of the sheet. The v2.0 MIDDLE reference (x 97 - 103, y 145 - 151, at
the centre of the answer grid) was REMOVED in v2.1: an outlined-but-empty
square floating mid-grid read as a printing defect, and TOP/BOTTOM refs plus
the strip's WHITE patch fully cover paper-white normalisation; brightness at
the grid's vertical middle is interpolated between the two remaining refs.

| Reference | Box x-range | Box y-range | Centre (x, y) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TOP | 70 - 76 | 284.5 - 290.5 | (73.0, 287.5) |
| BOTTOM | 34 - 40 | 7.5 - 13.5 | (37.0, 10.5) |

They sit in verified-empty margins: >= 1.5 mm from every printed element
and far from all timing marks, markers, bubbles and the QR quiet zone.

## 9. QR identity code

| Property | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Encoded payload | `SFGOMR1:DEMO-STUDENT-0001` |
| Version / EC level | Version 2 (25 x 25 modules), error correction M |
| Module size | 0.85 mm |
| Quiet zone | 4 modules = 3.40 mm on all four sides (enforced by a white plate) |
| Footprint incl. quiet zone | x 165.95 - 194.00, y 261.95 - 290.00 (28.05 x 28.05 mm) |
| Code box (module area) | x 169.35 - 190.60, y 265.35 - 286.60 |

Module addressing: row `r`, column `c` (both 0-based, row 0 at the top):
`x = 169.35 + c * 0.85`, top edge `y_top = 286.60 - r * 0.85`, so the module
occupies `y_top - 0.85 ... y_top` in bottom-left coordinates.

Payload versioning: `SFGOMR1:DEMO-STUDENT-0001` (25 bytes) is UNCHANGED in
v2.0 so existing scanner parsing keeps working. A version-tagged payload
`SFGOMR1:V2:DEMO-STUDENT-0001` (28 bytes) was verified to still fit
Version 2-M (28-byte data capacity) and to decode correctly, so it can be
adopted later with NO geometric change; adopting it only requires the
scanner payload parser to accept the extra segment (separate software
task). Until then, the layout version is carried by the human label
`SFG OMR 2.0` (bottom-left margin) and by `meta.layout_version` in the JSON.

## 14. Print and scanning notes

- **Print at 100 % scale** (no fit-to-page), plain black & white laser or
  inkjet; the layout tolerates typical printer skew of +-1 mm because all
  bubble detection is relative to the eight detected markers. The grey
  outlines print at ~35 %% halftone coverage, inside the reliable range of
  office printers; avoid 'economode' / draft modes, which thin light tones.
- **Perspective rectification.** Detect the four 7 mm solid corner squares,
  order them TL/TR/BR/BL, and map their centres to (9.5, 287.5), (200.5, 287.5),
  (200.5, 9.5), (9.5, 9.5) on the A4 millimetre plane
  (OpenCV `getPerspectiveTransform` + `warpPerspective`, e.g. 10 px/mm);
  then verify the warp with the four 4 mm edge-midpoint markers, whose
  centres must land at x = 105.0 (top / bottom) and y = 148.5 (left / right)
  within +-0.5 mm.
- **Bubble sampling (v2.0).** Classify each of the 200 answer bubbles by
  mean darkness INSIDE radius 1.0 mm, normalised against the local paper
  white (white references / local background): a filled bubble is dark
  inside, an empty one is white inside. The v1.1 'dark ring' signature is
  RETIRED - the printed outline is now light grey (core luminance ~166 of
  255), so ring darkness no longer identifies an empty bubble. The 16
  test-ID bubbles keep 4.0 mm diameter (interior radius 1.3 mm).
- **Template-aware subtraction (v2.0).** Warp the capture onto the A4 mm
  plane using the 8 markers, then subtract the known template: the
  preprinted grey outline appears in both images and cancels, leaving only
  student ink in the difference map. This tolerates +-1 mm registration
  error and +-10 %% print-density variation because the comparison happens
  per bubble interior (normalised), not pixel-for-pixel. `meta.template`
  and `meta.ink_model` in the JSON carry the machine-readable template
  version and ink spec.
- **Mark-type handling (v2.0).** Interior sampling classifies full fills,
  crosses and centred ticks; lightly shaded bubbles (>= 40 %% darker than
  local white) are flagged 'light' instead of binary; hollow re-traced
  circles need the optional annulus check (ink in the 1.2-2.2 mm ring
  beyond the template outline); partial fills land in a manual-review
  band; two or more dark interiors in one row report multi-selection; a
  long horizontal strike (continuous ink spanning more than two option
  widths at row height) is detected as a strike and suppresses per-bubble
  marks in that row; ink extending past a bubble boundary is handled by
  interior-only sampling plus the strike/overflow rules.
- **Timing marks.** Each of the 50 rectangles validates one row: after the
  warp its centre must land within +-0.4 mm of its row centre y; a growing
  offset down the page indicates vertical scaling error.
- **Avoiding false bubbles.** Restrict bubble blob detection to the option
  x-bands (LEFT 32.5 - 80.0 mm, RIGHT 123.5 - 171.0 mm). Timing marks
  (x 12.2 - 14.4 / 195.6 - 197.8), the edge-midpoint markers (x 103 - 107,
  x 6 - 10, x 200 - 204), the calibration strip (x 128 - 190,
  y 7.5 - 13.5), the two white references (top x 70 - 76, bottom
  x 34 - 40) and the four ArUco markers (section 7) all lie outside those
  bands and are rectangles or squares, so masking them first plus a
  circularity filter prevents any confusion with the 3.4 mm circular
  bubbles.
- **Illumination calibration (v2.1).** Sample the two white references
  (top / bottom) and the strip's white patch for local paper brightness;
  fit the seven strip tones (targets 0 / 32 / 64 / 128 / 191 / 223 / 255 at
  8-bit precision) to a tone curve; normalise bubble interiors with the
  local white level before thresholding. The patches DETECT uneven lighting, shadows and exposure
  drift (flag a capture when local white varies more than 10 %% between
  zones or the tone curve becomes non-monotonic); they do not remove glare
  or shadows after capture - recapture is the remedy.
- **ArUco registration (v2.1).** Run DICT_4X4_50 detection on the cropped
  page; exactly IDs 0-3 must be found and their corners must land within
  +-0.5 mm of the centres in section 7. Treat ArUco as a cross-check or as
  the recovery path when a corner marker is damaged; do not mix coordinate
  sources within one warp.
- **Blue variant.** The pale-blue outline (#8CAAE6, luminance-matched to
  the grey at ~168) behaves like the grey after RGB -> luminance
  conversion. It adds no accuracy by itself and is NOT recommended for
  monochrome printing or grayscale-only pipelines: hue information is
  lost, blue ballpoint ink is not separable by hue (only by lightness),
  and inkjet cyan rendering varies. Evaluate it only in the controlled
  RGB-camera comparison described in the v2.0 engineering report (plan G).
- **QR code.** Read from the footprint box above; the four-module quiet zone
  is guaranteed white, and the payload carries the portal ID for identity
  cross-checking (`SFGOMR1:DEMO-STUDENT-0001`).
- **Verification render.** `pdftoppm -r 300 -png SFG_OMR_Lab_Minimal.pdf`
  produces the 2480 x 3508 px reference image used by the QC pipeline.
